HI, I'M YEKRA (iq-ra)

Designing Robust Analog ICs and Efficient Power Converters

HELLO.

I am Md Yekra Rahman, a PhD student in Electrical Engineering with a concentration in circuits and analog IC design. I am currently pursuing my doctoral studies at the University of Missouri–Columbia, USA, after completing a PhD-track program at the University of Texas at Dallas (TxACE). I obtained my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (Electronics major) from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My technical interests lie at the intersection of analog/mixed-signal IC design and power electronics, with a particular focus on PMICs, switch-mode power conversion, wide-bandgap devices (SiC/GaN), and EMI-aware converter design. I have hands-on experience in transistor-level design, simulation, and verification of analog and mixed-signal building blocks—including op-amps, PLLs, bandgap references, LDOs, and gate-driver circuits—using Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, and gm/ID methodology.

In parallel, I work on the design and validation of power conversion systems across schematic, layout-aware simulation, and PCB implementation, with an emphasis on reproducible design workflows, high-frequency behavior, and practical measurement considerations. I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, mentoring students in CMOS IC design, SPICE-based simulation, and laboratory validation.

My long-term goal is to pursue a research- and design-oriented career in analog and power IC development, contributing to robust, efficient, and scalable integrated solutions for modern electronic and energy systems.
