HELLO.

I am Md Yekra Rahman, a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Missouri–Columbia (M-I-Z!), specializing in analog IC design and power management. I am currently actively seeking Summer 2026 internship opportunities where I can apply my expertise in circuit design and verification to solve real-world power challenges.

My technical focus bridges the gap between analog/mixed-signal design and power electronics applications. I have deep experience designing PMICs, LDOs, and gate drivers for wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) systems. My workflow relies on industry-standard tools including Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, and the gm/ID methodology to deliver robust, verified designs.

Beyond simulation, I bring hands-on validation experience. From layout-aware simulation to PCB implementation and lab measurement, I focus on reproducible workflows and EMI-aware design. As a Graduate Teaching Assistant, I also hone my ability to communicate complex technical concepts effectively—a skill I am eager to bring to a collaborative engineering team.
